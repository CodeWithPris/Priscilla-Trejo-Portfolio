About

Hello, friend.

My name is Priscilla N. Trejo, and I enjoy the puzzling process of coding.

I've always been fascinated by technology since childhood, from building Lego robots to exploring PC software and components. Inspired by the children's book series "Warriors", I began experimenting with website builders like Weebly and Webs, creating forums and mobile-responsive sites by the age of 10. This love for the ever-changing world of technology has inspired me to attend Indiana University of Indianapolis to obtain my Bachelor's in Computer Science. I am currently a sophomore and have an estimated graduation date of Spring 2029.

In highschool, I was a member of the FIRST Robotics Competition Team #5484, where I helped design early prototypes of our robot and contributed to the development of specific robot functions. I also assisted with the assembly of the drivetrain frame and worked closely with teammates to troubleshoot both mechanical and programming challenges. Beyond the technical aspects, I supported the team through graphic design, drawings, and promotional materials, and reached out to local businesses to help secure funding. These difficult, yet rewarding experiences taught me collaboration, problem-solving, and inspired me to begin my journey of learning code. I am now Alumni of the program and give back to the FIRST community through yearly event volunteering and monetary donations.

On top of everything else, I am also an active member of local Web Development groups, such as freeCodeCamp Indy, where I organize weekly virtual events and have spoken about various projects of mine.

Aetheria: Wrath of the Aetherwyrm

This is a text-based adventure game built in Python. Players navigate locations, collect items, and attempt to win by restoring their power before confronting man’s village. The game features:

  • Inventory management so players can collect and track items needed to win
  • Navigate the land of Aetheria with valid direction options
  • Simple win/lose conditions means the player understands the game is dependent on both player choices and items collected
  • Input validation ensures the player enter valid responses and is able to quit at anytime by simply typing quit
  • Since the game is text-based, typing animation can enhance the storytelling and provide immersion
